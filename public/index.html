GPX Forge

Any map link → a GPX your head unit understands

1 · Bring in a route

What can I paste?
  • Google Maps — share a route, or copy the address-bar URL of a directions page. Short maps.app.goo.gl links work too.
  • Apple Maps — share → Copy Link.
  • OpenStreetMap, Bing, Waze, BRouter links.
  • Any other site (Komoot, Strava, RideWithGPS, plotaroute) — export GPX there and drop the file here.
  • Coordinates or place names, one per line.

Google and Apple provide no route-export API, so the app reads the waypoints out of your link and re-plans the route on the OpenStreetMap cycling network — which gives better bike routing than the original anyway.

    Free at openrouteservice.org/dev/#/signup — 2 000 routes/day, and it is the only engine that returns surface data with the route. Stored only in this browser.

    Used when the routing engine does not supply elevation itself.

    Analysis tuning

    Raising the ascent threshold discards more DEM noise. 3 m matches what Strava and Garmin report; 0 m gives the raw, over-counted figure.
